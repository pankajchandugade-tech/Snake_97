### Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Step back into the golden age of mobile gaming with **Retro Snake**, the most authentic recreation of the legendary 1990s monochrome phone game!

Relive the nostalgic thrill of controlling the pixel snake on an authentic dot-matrix LCD screen, complete with iconic monophonic square-wave beeps, physical-style tactile keypad controls, and classic bonus insect creatures.

### 🎮 AUTHENTIC RETRO FEATURES
* **Dot-Matrix LCD Display:** Enjoy the iconic green phosphor backlight, authentic pixel grid, and retro styling of the classic 1997 mobile era.
* **Dual Play Styles:**
  • **Phone Frame Mode:** Features a nostalgic curved mobile phone casing with physical-style keypad buttons (2-4-6-8, NAVI key, and soft keys).
  • **Arcade Touch Mode:** Maximizes the LCD screen with an ergonomic on-screen D-pad for modern handheld play.
* **Responsive Swipe & Touch:** Swipe in any direction directly across the LCD display for instant, fluid turns.
* **Classic 8-Bit Audio:** Nostalgic monophonic square-wave beeps for turns, eating, bonus creatures, and classic game-over tunes.
* **Haptic Feedback:** Feel every turn and dot with subtle tactile vibration.

### 🐛 5 CLASSIC MAZES
1. **No Walls (Wrap-around):** Pass through screen boundaries freely.
2. **Box (Border):** Classic solid outer perimeter walls.
3. **Tunnel:** Top and bottom corridors with center pillars.
4. **Mill:** Four obstacle crosses testing your reflex turns.
5. **Rails:** Dual horizontal barrier tracks for advanced navigation.

### ⚡ 9 SPEED LEVELS & BONUS BEETLES
* Adjust your pace from relaxed retro speeds (Level 1) to lightning-fast reflexes (Level 9).
* Every 5 regular food items eaten, a crawling bonus beetle appears with a countdown timer! Catch it before time expires for massive score boosts.

### 🏆 HIGH SCORES & THEMES
* Individual high score tracking for each maze, saved directly on your device.
* Custom LCD screen backlights: Classic 3310 Green, 3210 Amber / Orange, 1100 Ice Blue, and Monochrome Gray.
* 100% offline gameplay with zero internet required.

Rediscover the timeless casual gaming masterpiece that started it all!
