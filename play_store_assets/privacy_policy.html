Privacy Policy for Retro Snake

Last updated: October 2026

No Personal Data Collected

1. Overview

Retro Snake ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a casual retro arcade game. We prioritize your privacy and design our applications to function without collecting any personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Information Collection and Storage

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All gameplay progress, high scores, speed preferences, and theme choices are stored exclusively on your local device using standard on-device storage (HTML5 localStorage / Android local preferences).

3. Device Permissions

  • VIBRATION (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used exclusively to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay turns and button presses. No sensitive data is accessed or collected.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not currently employ third-party tracking, analytics, or profiling SDKs.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via your designated developer support email.
